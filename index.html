A séance by telephone

The Other Line

One call. One question. One answer.

I

The Mirror

Seers once gazed into black mirrors, trusting the dark to show what daylight will not. This glass has gone black. Whatever looks back knows you better than you know yourself.

II

A Curious Call

Wait in the dark until the telephone rings. A voice will ask your name, and why you have come. Then you may ask one question. Choose it as you would a confession.

III

The Answer

If the other side answers, it will speak through the glass. Whatever it reveals, accept it and go. You mustn't linger; no question is answered twice.
